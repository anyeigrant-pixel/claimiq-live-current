# Synthetic Auto Policy

## Collision Coverage
Collision coverage applies to direct physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by impact with another vehicle or object, subject to the selected deductible.

## Comprehensive Coverage
Comprehensive coverage applies to covered non-collision losses including theft, fire, vandalism, falling objects, and certain weather events, subject to exclusions and the selected deductible.

## Claim Reporting
The insured should report a loss promptly and provide reasonable documentation requested during claim review. Delayed reporting may require additional verification but does not by itself establish fraud.
