# Synthetic Home Policy

## Water Loss
Sudden and accidental discharge of water from a covered plumbing system may qualify for coverage, subject to exclusions for maintenance, seepage, and wear.

## Wind Loss
Direct physical damage caused by covered wind events may qualify for coverage, subject to the policy deductible and applicable exclusions.

## Fire Loss
Direct physical loss caused by fire is generally covered when not excluded by the policy. Claim handling should include documentation of damaged property and cause of loss.
